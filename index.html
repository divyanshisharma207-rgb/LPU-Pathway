Help & Accessibility Guide

Using with TalkBack (Android)

  • Swipe right/left to navigate between controls
  • Double-tap to activate buttons and links
  • Use the route form then swipe down to hear directions
  • Tap "Read Aloud" button to hear the full route
  • All map features have text alternatives

Using with VoiceOver (iOS/Mac)

  • Swipe right to move forward through elements
  • Double-tap anywhere to activate focused item
  • Use the Rotor (two-finger rotation) for headings
  • Use the "Read Aloud" button for spoken directions
  • Live regions announce route updates automatically

Keyboard Navigation

  • Tab — Move to next interactive element
  • Shift + Tab — Move to previous element
  • Enter / Space — Activate buttons
  • Arrow keys — Navigate radio button options
  • Skip links at the top take you to main areas

Step-Free Route Guide

  • Always choose "Step-Free" if you use a wheelchair
  • Routes use ramps and lifts only — zero stairs
  • If a lift is broken, the route auto-updates
  • Accessible routes may be slightly longer in distance
  • Report a blocked ramp or lift using the Report tab

Campus Stories & Visual Orientation

Photo tours, architectural highlights, and essential navigation tips for famous LPU spots to help new students feel oriented.

Visual Orientation & Landmark Stories

Short visual tours and architectural highlights of famous LPU landmarks to help new students and visitors feel at home.

Student Life
45s Tour

Baldev Raj Mittal Unipolis

"The Amphitheatre of Dreams"

Baldev Raj Mittal Unipolis is LPU’s iconic 10,000-capacity open amphitheatre. It hosts our star-studded Youth Vibe concerts, celebrity nights, and cultural convocations. Its wide paved ramps make it 100% accessible from all sides.

Orientation Tip: The central boulevard passing through Unipolis is the smoothest, step-free walking route between academic blocks and student hostels.
Academics
50s Tour

Shanti Devi Mittal Central Library

"Northern India’s 5-Storey Knowledge Citadel"

Spanning five immense floors, the Central Library houses over 1.5 million volumes, RFID automated checkout kiosks, soundproof discussion pods, and peaceful air-conditioned reading halls.

Orientation Tip: Need absolute silence for exam preparation? Head straight to the quiet reading rooms on Floors 2 and 3.
Dining & Retail
40s Tour

Uni-Mall Shopping & Food Plaza

"The Campus Downtown & Social Hub"

Uni-Mall brings multi-cuisine food courts, Domino’s, Subway, national bank branches, ATMs, stationery superstores, and student boutiques together in an air-conditioned lifestyle complex.

Orientation Tip: During summer heat or monsoon showers, use the covered ground corridor connecting Block 34 directly into Uni-Mall.
Sports & Fitness
45s Tour

Indoor Sports Stadium

"Olympic-Standard Athletic Marvel"

Features an Olympic-size heated swimming pool, 12 professional wooden badminton courts, glass-back squash arenas, an indoor shooting range, and modern gyms.

Orientation Tip: Students can access the gym and pool free during scheduled university sports timings with their student ID.
Campus Entry
35s Tour

Main Gate 1 & Sandstone Boulevard

"The Gateway to Lovely Professional University"

The grand red sandstone archway stands proudly on the Grand Trunk (GT) Road. It features dedicated transit bays for university buses, e-rickshaws, and free accessible wheelchair golf carts.

Orientation Tip: Free university wheelchair carts depart from Gate 1 every three minutes directly to classroom blocks.
Technology
50s Tour

Block 32 & 34 Tech Quadrangle

"The Innovation & Coding Powerhouse"

The computing epicenter of campus, housing advanced AI laboratories, autonomous robotics clusters, iOS development centres, and 24/7 student hackathon incubators.

Orientation Tip: Lift L-32A on the north side is equipped with voice floor announcements and provides step-free access to all 5 floors.
